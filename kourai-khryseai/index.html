I built a team of ten AI agents that writes software with you

What I didAn open-source team of ten AI agents, each its own server. They plan, code, test, review and commit, and show their reasoning as they go.

How the agents talk to each other

ProtocolA2A, the open agent-to-agent protocol: each agent is its own HTTP server, so any one can be deployed alone, tested alone or replaced
The routeHephaestus calls one specialist at a time and waits for its result before calling the next
StreamingEach specialist streams its reasoning back as it works; the hosts show it live
ToolsMCP for tools and files; A2A only between agents
Built withPython; LiteLLM between the agents and the models; OpenTelemetry for the traces
