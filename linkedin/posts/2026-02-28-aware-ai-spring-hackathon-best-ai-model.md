Won Best AI Model at the AWARE-AI Spring 2026 Hackathon for a real-time classifier that maps gesture EMG into musical control. 🤓

24 hours. The brief: build something that uses your body's signals to control a system. Our team picked music 🎶 Wave your hand, play a note.

Pipeline: BioRadio biosignals → feature extraction → ML classifier → control system. Streamed live over Lab Streaming Layer (LSL).

Lesson: 24h hackathons reward whoever picks the right small problem fast and ships it end-to-end. Scope discipline beats architecture choices when the clock is the constraint.
