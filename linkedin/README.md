# LinkedIn

Every post AJ has published, with its text, date and an impressions snapshot, plus the checklist each new post is written against. Start a new post from the closest old one in [`posts/`](posts/) and this checklist, the way a new docs site starts from an old one.

## Checklist (November 2026)

The sources and how strong each one is are in [research-2026-11.md](research-2026-11.md). Only two points come from LinkedIn itself: the feed ranks with LLMs, and it filters engagement bait. The rest is the best available practice, not a rule.

**Before writing**
- Read the two or three closest posts in `posts/` and their Details.
- Read the code or results behind every claim.

**The text**
- The first line is the hook: concrete and specific, about 140 characters at most, with no blank line before the "see more" fold.
- Never open with "thrilled" or "humbled to announce".
- 1,200 to 1,800 characters, short paragraphs.
- Substance over bait: a finding, a number, a design decision. No "Agree?", "Thoughts?", polls or "like if".
- A closing question only if it is specific and technical; none is better than a generic one.
- Plain text only: no Unicode bold or italic letters (screen readers spell them out, and search and ranking cannot read them).
- No em dashes. No defensive lines. No claim a selection could inflate (for example, a count of only your own good runs).
- At most 3 relevant hashtags, at the end.

**People**
- Teammates who built it with you: add them as **collaborators** (up to 5; they accept, and the post shows on their profiles).
- Thank the team as a team, by name. Never list who did what (AJ, 2026-11-12).
- People you thank: tag them, few and only real participants. Check that each tag is the right person (affiliation, 1st-degree). Government and LAS staff: first names only, no tag; many prefer to stay private online (AJ, 2026-11-12).
- Never credit someone who was not on the team.

**Media**
- Images 1:1 square, 1200x1200, padded rather than cropped. The first image sets the grid crop.
- Only images you own or may use; verify the hardware model or product in any photo.
- Alt text on every image (about 120 to 300 characters; transcribe the text in charts).

**Links**
- Put links in the first comment, posted right after the post. Say so in the post ("Code, site and the full deck are in the first comment").
- If a link stays in the body, dismiss the preview card. LinkedIn attaches it again after edits, and it blocks adding images.

**When and after**
- Post Tuesday to Thursday, about 8 to 9 AM ET.
- Stay for the first hour and answer comments with substance.
- Verify the live post: text, tags, collaborators, images, the first comment.

**Old posts**
- Editing an old post does not bring back its reach. Fix only accuracy, broken links, accessibility and the style rules above.
- An attached document's title cannot be edited after posting.

## Workflow

1. Draft from the checklist and the closest old posts, with the facts checked against the repos.
2. AJ reads it in context and corrects it. A correction is context for a fix, not text to paste in.
3. Compose in the browser: text, collaborators, tags (typed as @ mentions and picked from the list), images with alt text.
4. Check the composer preview (crops, preview card), then post on AJ's word.
5. Post the first comment with the links, then verify the live post.
6. Add the post to `posts/` with a Details section: people, images, links, what was cut and why.
